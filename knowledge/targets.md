# What Khushi looks for

## Location priority

1. **Ranchi, Jharkhand** (on-site or hybrid): highest priority.
2. **Other Jharkhand** cities, when the role fits.
3. **Remote India / remote anywhere**, when it can realistically pay.

## Roles that fit

AI / AI intern / AI assistant / AI automation (intern or executive), automation executive, AI operations,
operations executive with tech, technical support, IT executive, web developer, junior web developer, Shopify
developer, e-commerce executive or operations, digital marketing executive, Meta Ads / paid media, Google Ads,
marketing automation, social media executive or manager, content creator, CRM executive, technical executive,
software support, junior developer, freelance developer, AI or automation freelancer.

## Freelance signals (FAST money)

Posts where a business or founder needs: a website, Shopify work, AI automation, a chatbot, WhatsApp automation,
a CRM, n8n / Claude / GPT workflows, marketing automation, Meta or Google Ads, content or social media automation,
or a small development fix.

## Skip (don't comment, don't contact)

- BPO / call-centre / telecalling, field sales, sales-only targets, delivery, warehouse, construction, civil,
  interior design, teaching, HR or recruiter roles, banking/insurance sales, "male only" voice process.
- Roles that require 5+ years, a specific degree as a must, or senior/lead titles.
- Unpaid volunteer roles (unless Sumit asks).
- Agencies or freelancers advertising their own services, polls, "job scam" warnings, course sellers.
- Anything asking for money to apply.

## Scoring

- **HOT:** fits the roles above **and** (Ranchi/Jharkhand on-site or hybrid **or** a freelance/remote project
  that matches his projects), posted within ~10 days, and the poster or an application path is reachable.
- **WARM:** fits, but older, remote with weak pay signals, or asks for a bit more experience than he has.
- **SKIP:** anything in the skip list or not a hiring/need post.

## Payment speed

- **FAST:** freelance / project / contract, "need someone now", small fixes.
- **MEDIUM:** job or internship with immediate joining.
- **SLOW:** long hiring process, big company, campus-style drives.
